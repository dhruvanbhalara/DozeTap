## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-25

### Added
- **Screen Timeout Controls**: Easily change how long your device screen stays on before turning off.
- **Quick Settings Tile**: Toggle screen timeout directly from your Android notification shade.
- **Home Screen Widgets**: Switch screen off durations with a single tap using home screen widgets.
- **Custom Timeouts**: Set custom display durations beyond standard Android settings presets.
- **Shizuku Support**: Grant required settings permissions using Shizuku without needing a computer or manual ADB commands.
- **Material You Theme**: Dynamic theme styling and dark mode support matching your system preferences.
- **Privacy & Offline**: Zero network permissions, zero tracking, and completely offline operation.
